Blackball Pool Rules
Objective

Pot all seven of your group's balls (reds or yellows), then pot the black ball in a nominated pocket to win the frame.

The break
  • The balls are racked in a triangle with the black in the centre and a red and a yellow ball in the back corners.
  • The breaker plays from behind the baulk line and must pot a ball or drive at least two balls to a cushion.
  • If the break is illegal, the opponent may ask for a re-rack or take the table as it stands.
  • If the black is potted on the break, the frame is re-racked (or the black is re-spotted).
Choosing groups
  • The table is "open" until a player legally pots a red or yellow after the break. That player takes that colour and their opponent takes the other.
  • While the table is open, a player may hit either colour first, but not the black.
Taking a shot
  • The first ball the white touches must be one of your own group's balls.
  • After contact, a ball must be potted or any ball must reach a cushion.
  • If you pot one of your own balls, you continue your visit.
  • If you pot nothing, or commit a foul, your visit ends.
  • You may only play the black once all your own balls are potted.
Fouls

It is a foul if you:

  • Pot the white.
  • Hit an opponent's ball or the black first.
  • Miss every ball.
  • Fail to pot a ball or send a ball to a cushion after contact.
  • Move a ball by any means other than a legal shot.
  • Hit the white more than once in a shot.
  • Have both feet off the floor when playing a shot.
After a foul
  • Your opponent gets two visits.
  • Your opponent has the white in hand and may place it anywhere on the table.
  • Any balls potted on the foul stay down.
The black
  • Once all your balls are potted, nominate a pocket for the black before you play it.
  • Potting the black in the nominated pocket wins the frame.
Losing the frame

You lose the frame if you:

  • Pot the black before clearing all your own balls.
  • Pot the black and the white in the same shot.
  • Pot the black in a pocket other than the one you nominated.
  • Knock the black off the table.

These are the commonly played blackball rules. House rules vary, so agree any differences before you start.
